comparison`. The answer gets rewritten when it changes; the question does not, so the title and every citation of it
survive.

## Context
Describe the context and problem we are trying to solve. What are the constraints, requirements, and background details?

## Alternatives
What other options were considered, and why were they ruled out? Where this ADR is being rewritten because the decision
changed, the decision it replaces moves here with what made it fail — nothing else records that it was tried.

## Decision
What is the proposed decision, action, or design? Why is this the best choice? Present tense: how the system is built,
not what was once resolved.

## Trade-offs
What are the positive benefits and negative side effects of this decision?

## Consequences
What are the long-term impacts, risks, or subsequent actions required as a result of this decision?
