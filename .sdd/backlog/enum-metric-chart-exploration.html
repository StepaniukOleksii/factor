Enum Metric visualization

Three candidate forms for charting an Enum Metric, then the recommended one under the two bucket regimes it has to survive. Drawn at true trend-card size (380×108) in the app's own theme. The green ramp is the validated ordinal ramp, not hand-picked.

The three candidate forms — same data, mood at 1M

One record per bucket, so every bucket has a single unambiguous value. This is the easy regime, and all three forms are readable in it — which is exactly why it is the wrong regime to choose on.

A — Swimlane. Identity is position; hue restates the declared order.
mood
B — Stacked column. Identity is hue alone, and needs a legend the card has no room for.
mood
C — Mode line. Slopes imply a distance between categories that does not exist.
The regime that decides it — the same Metric at 1Y

At 1Y each bucket is 30 days wide and folds a dozen records spanning every value. Form C has to pick a winner and silently discards the rest. Form A gives each bucket a mark in every lane it touched, sized to that value's share — so a mixed month reads as mixed, and a settled one still reads as a solid run.

A at 1Y — the drift from low to high stays visible, and March/April read as settled.
mood
C at 1Y — twelve months of mixture flattened to four steps. Every month here folds ~12 records.
Where it stops working

Lane geometry and the color ramp cap out at the same place. Five lanes in a 108px card leaves 17px each, and the validated green ramp holds five steps but fails at seven — adjacent lightness gaps collapse below the 0.06 floor. Past five values the card has to grow with the lane count rather than squeeze.

energy
Five lanes — the practical ceiling at the current card height.
