mixed metrics at the default 1M, with the right-hand marker's popover open — as it arrives, folded. The marks sit in a 20px band between the Metric's name and the plot, so no tap target reaches a chart and the charts keep every tap they had; a card reserves that band only where its window holds an Event to mark. Three seeded Events fall in a 30-day window — repeated 21 days back, this week 3 days back and today 3 hours back — so three rules stand at the same place on every card and read straight down the column. Only two marks stand above them: the right-hand pair fall about 28px apart, inside one target's width, so they share a handle, and its mark is a pill spanning both their rules rather than a square on one of them. Every rule runs up through the band to the mark that answers for it. Four of the observation's eight cards are shown: sparse, hourly, yearly and flag are omitted for length and carry the same three rules. The popover's own layouts are in event-marker-popover.html beside this file.
